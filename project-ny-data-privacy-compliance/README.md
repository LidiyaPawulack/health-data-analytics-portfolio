# New York Data Privacy & Compliance Risk Analysis (2024–2025)

This project analyzes publicly reported healthcare data breach incidents in New York to assess trends in data privacy risk and compliance exposure.

## Data Source
U.S. Department of Health & Human Services (HHS) public breach reporting data.

## Methods
- Data cleaning and validation
- SQL-derived field creation (Year extraction)
- Pivot table analysis
- Descriptive trend analysis
- Data visualization

## Key Findings
- Incident volume increased from 2024 to 2025, driven primarily by Hacking/IT incidents.
- Unauthorized Access/Disclosure incidents increased in prevalence despite lower absolute counts.
- Findings indicate expanding cybersecurity exposure within New York healthcare entities.
